Today marks the first day back in the office following the National Day holiday, though some engineers have yet to return.

1. I have been studying Gwynne Shotwell’s management philosophy at SpaceX. Her model relies on "coach-engineers" rather than having pure managers on the team.

1. I deeply admire the concept of an all-engineer team, and I aim to cultivate that same culture within my own team.
